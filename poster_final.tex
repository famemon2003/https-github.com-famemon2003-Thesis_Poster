	\section{Two decisions, one disconnected evidence base}

	A monitoring programme must answer two linked questions: \textcolor{ICLBlue}{where should measurement effort go}, and \textcolor{ICLBlue}{what should make the measurement}. What has been measured, what a model can be trusted to predict and what an instrument can be relied upon to detect are rarely joined.

	\smalltext{\textbf{Scope.} India is the principal empirical application. Kenya and Malawi are bounded local comparators and support no national estimate; Bangladesh and Ghana appear only as literature-grounded applications of the decision logic, with no model transferred to either.\\[10pt]
	\textbf{What the records could support.} Six India parameters reached an accepted analytical population. Turbidity and \textit{E.\ coli} reached none, so no baseline was claimed for either. Of 145 state-by-parameter cells with recorded cadence, none is revisited more often than annually at its median --- a monitoring-design limit no model can repair.}

	\boldsection{A recommendation is only as defensible as the evidence layer beneath it.}

	\closingnote{Hosmer, D., Lemeshow, S. \& Sturdivant, R. (2013). \textit{Applied logistic regression}. 3rd edn. Hoboken: Wiley. \quad Hu, X., Dai, M., Sun, J. \& Sunderland, E. (2023). \textit{Current Environmental Health Reports}.\\[10pt]

	\section{Random splitting overstated performance}

	Seven tasks, three validation designs. Geographic blocking is what a programme extending into an unmonitored district should plan against.

	\begin{figure}[H]
		\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{poster/P2_leakage_safe_validation.pdf}
	\end{figure}

	\fignote{\textbf{P2.} Values from Table 4.3 and Section 4.3 of the submitted thesis.\\[10pt]
	The 0.1701 optimism decomposes into \textbf{0.0708} removed by grouping repeat visits to a station, and a further \textbf{0.0993} revealed only by geographic separation.\\[10pt]
	A scoped label-shuffle control collapsed spatial performance to \textbf{0.501} (95\% interval 0.482 to 0.520): recoverable signal, not a label-independent spatial artefact.\\[10pt]
	Same-site history (Model B) was higher on all six shared tasks --- a \textcolor{ICLBlue}{random-row channel comparison, not geographic generalisation}.}

	\midnum{2.01×}\hspace{1.3cm}\midnum{2.40×}

	\fignote{Confirmatory-test enrichment over the untargeted expectation at the \textbf{recorded operating point}: conductivity, 5,966 tests finding 2,838 exceeding sites against 1,410; TDS, 265 tests finding 154 against 64.2. Not usable for the other four parameters.\\[10pt]
	\textbf{Rajasthan, worked through.} 12,349 conductivity records at 1,641 sites, 54.06\% above the 1,500\,\textmu S/cm project convention --- a record-level signal, not population exposure. Rank transfer sequences the sampling; the calibration slopes forbid reading any score as a local probability; four products carry stated capability and none passes the evidence gate. Terminal decision: a conditional class shortlist, product-level abstention.}

	\section{Capability was common; product evidence was not}

	\bignum{0 of 23}

	\fignote{exact commercial products cleared the eleven-criterion evidence gate. Of \textbf{1,242} product--criterion cells (23 products × 54 criteria), \textbf{223} carry an exact-product document and only \textbf{9} carry independent evidence. The 33-entry landscape is separate: 23 products, 8 reference methods, 2 emerging classes. \textcolor{ICLBlue}{This is abstention, not poor performance --- missing documentation remains unknown evidence.}}

	\begin{figure}[H]
		\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{poster/P4_next_action_26_contexts.pdf}
	\end{figure}

	\fignote{\textbf{P4.} Direct counts across 26 parameter-context decisions in India, Kenya and Malawi, from Figure 4.14 of the submitted thesis. Not a score, and not 26 countries or sites.\\[10pt]
	\textbf{What resource-constrained monitoring still needs.} Neither cost nor energy per valid result can be derived for any of the 23 products, so neither saving can be priced. Threshold-near accuracy, operator effect, invalid tests, reliability, reagent stability and persistent site, sample, time and method identifiers are the recurring gaps. Of sixteen research priorities, only two concern sensor design.}
